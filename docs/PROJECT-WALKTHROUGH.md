# TerraTrace project walkthrough

This is a hands-on project guide. GitHub publication and hackathon submission remain deferred; no video is produced.

1. **Open the city twin.** Choose a featured city. Check the weather source time and map capture date. The mapped district is a bounded sample, not the complete city.
2. **Inspect the district.** Expand the map, pan and zoom, switch between plan and isometric views, and search for a building. Inspect its footprint, source tags and height provenance.
3. **Compare an intervention.** Change roof treatments and proposed trees. Keep the assumed demand, grid factor, performance and transferred cooling assumptions visible. Read energy and temperature comparisons as hypothetical scenarios.
4. **Inspect roof surveys and shade.** The survey ranks building capacity under editable usable-area assumptions. The animated shade timeline illustrates ground shadows; it does not change PV output or establish observed thermal comfort.
5. **Save and compare strategies.** Name the working plan and save up to four alternatives. Choose two map sides, inspect the tradeoff plot and exact results table, then apply a saved alternative to the working plan. All comparisons share one captured forecast, map and reference assumptions. Use Recalculate all to adopt current data for every plan together. Export or seal the full comparison.
6. **Plan the site survey.** Open Evidence and compare height-source categories. Inspect a footprint-density cell or search for a building by name, use or OSM ID. Add it to the survey list and export the checklist. Missing tags remain survey questions; a saved entry is not a completed survey.
7. **Read the research.** The library links nine papers and eight organizer-verified winning projects. Each explains the design pattern used and the limits of transferring its conclusions.
8. **Preserve evidence.** Seal a city scenario, strategy comparison or environmental snapshot, export it, and import it into the verifier. Changing a committed value causes verification to fail. Previously exported receipts remain supported. Optional Sepolia anchoring requires a real deployed registry and wallet confirmation.
9. **Review the next features.** The roadmap documents proposed rainwater capture, measured-change and later shaded-route tools, with data requirements and completion criteria. These three are not implemented.
