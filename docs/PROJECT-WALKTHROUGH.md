# TerraTrace project walkthrough

This is a hands-on project guide. GitHub publication and hackathon submission remain deferred; no video is produced.

1. **Open the city twin.** Choose a featured city. Check the weather source time and map capture date. The mapped district is a bounded sample, not the complete city.
2. **Inspect the district.** Expand the map, pan and zoom, switch between plan and isometric views, and search for a building. Inspect its footprint, source tags and height provenance.
3. **Compare an intervention.** Change roof treatments and proposed trees. Keep the assumed demand, grid factor, performance and transferred cooling assumptions visible. Read energy and temperature comparisons as hypothetical scenarios.
4. **Inspect roof surveys and shade.** The survey ranks building capacity under editable usable-area assumptions. The animated shade timeline illustrates ground shadows; it does not change PV output or establish observed thermal comfort.
5. **Read the research.** The library links nine papers and eight organizer-verified winning projects. Each explains the design pattern used and the limits of transferring its conclusions.
6. **Preserve evidence.** Seal a city scenario or an environmental snapshot, export it, and import it into the verifier. Changing a committed value causes verification to fail. Previously exported receipts remain supported. Optional Sepolia anchoring requires a real deployed registry and wallet confirmation.
7. **Review the next features.** The feature roadmap specifies a strategy lab, evidence lens, rainwater capture scenario, measurement workbench and later shaded routes. Their data requirements, visual interactions and completion criteria are documented; they are not yet implemented.
