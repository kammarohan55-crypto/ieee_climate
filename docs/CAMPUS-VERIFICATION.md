For the example's 50% water / 5% sun-reduction goals, the bounded comparison selects one 5 m³ tank and two conceptual shelters. This is a public-data proposal with unknown prices and unsurveyed sites, not a completed installation or measured environmental benefit.

## Practical limits

No verified local LiDAR clip is bundled. Heights and crown dimensions retain mapped, derived or explicitly assumed provenance; the OSM tree inventory is partial. Pedestrian street centerlines are access proxies rather than a complete sidewalk/accessibility survey. The rain gauge is approximately 10 km away. Model-member spread is not calibrated probability, and the AI reference buildings are anonymized US education buildings rather than London campus meters.

Desktop and 390 px mobile layouts were inspected in the browser. A universal frame-rate claim has not been established across hardware. The existing framework emits middleware-deprecation and large-chunk notices; the 3D scene, demand workbench and passport interface are loaded separately.
